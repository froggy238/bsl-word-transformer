DATA NOTICE — third-party video content (examination use only)
===============================================================

This archive is an MSc dissertation submission. It contains third-party video
material included SOLELY to allow examiners to verify the reported experiments.
It must not be redistributed, published, or reused outside the examination of
this dissertation.

1) data/raw_videos/  (293 clips)
   BSL dictionary clips located through SignBSL.com; copyright remains with the
   contributing organisations recorded per clip in data/metadata.csv (source
   URLs included). Downloaded politely, sequentially, for non-commercial
   academic research. The project's public repository ships only derived
   landmark coordinates and source URLs, never these videos.

2) data/test_videos_signbank/  (100 clips)
   Citation-form sign clips from BSL SignBank (bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk),
   copyright UCL / DCAL. Downloaded with UCL's permission for
   research use in this project (permission correspondence archived in the
   ethics appendix). Used exclusively as a held-out evaluation set. Full
   per-file provenance: data/signbank_metadata.csv and
   data/signbank_gloss_map*.csv.

Derived data (data/landmarks*, data/test_landmarks*) are MediaPipe Holistic
landmark coordinates only — no imagery — and are shareable under the project's
data statement (see README.md, "Ethics and data statement").
